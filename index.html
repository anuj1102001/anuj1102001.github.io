EXPLORATORY ANALYSISJUL 2026

Half a million transactions.
One bigger picture.

Finding product, customer, and geographic patterns in e-commerce sales. A study in turning a large dataset into a useful business narrative.

PythonPandasNumPy
Inside the project +

The question

How do products, customers, geography, and time shape sales performance?

The work

Cleaned transactions, investigated missing values, removed duplicates, and validated records. Engineered revenue and time-based features, then explored product, customer, geographic, and monthly patterns.

The output

Business-focused visualizations and insights from £10.25M+ in analyzed revenue, spanning 3,900+ products, 4,300+ customers, and 38 countries.

These figures describe the scope of the resume project, not revenue generated or business uplift.
